# SR 26-2 Model Risk Management Mapping and GenAI Scope Note

## What changed

On April 17, 2026, the Federal Reserve, OCC, and FDIC issued revised interagency model-risk guidance. Federal Reserve SR 26-2 supersedes and replaces SR 11-7 and SR 21-8. The guidance emphasizes a tailored, risk-based approach and is expected to be most relevant to banking organizations with more than $30 billion in total assets, while recognizing circumstances where smaller organizations may have significant model risk.

## Critical GenAI scope limitation

The attachment explicitly states that **generative AI and agentic AI models are not within the scope of the guidance**. It also states that an organization's governance should determine appropriate controls for tools, processes, or systems outside the document's scope. Therefore:

- do not state that SR 26-2 directly requires GenAI validation;
- do not carry forward legacy claims that every material GenAI system is an SR 11-7 model;
- determine separately whether a non-generative statistical or quantitative component is within scope; and
- use model-risk principles for GenAI only as an internal governance choice or where another requirement incorporates them.

## Informative principle mapping

| SR 26-2 principle | Potential internal AI governance use |
|---|---|
| Risk based on inherent risk, exposure, purpose, and use | [AI risk tiering](../governance/risk-tiering/ai-risk-tiering-framework.md) |
| Effective challenge | Risk-proportionate independent review of evidence and limitations |
| Development aligned to purpose and use | Requirements, architecture rationale, data, assumptions, and testing |
| Validation and outcomes analysis | [Testing framework](../testing/testing-framework/enterprise-genai-testing.md) and production evaluation |
| Ongoing monitoring | Thresholds, changes, limitations, and remediation |
| Governance, inventory, documentation | Ownership, lifecycle records, dependencies, and evidence |
| Vendor products | Vendor understanding, use-case testing, monitoring, and customization review |
